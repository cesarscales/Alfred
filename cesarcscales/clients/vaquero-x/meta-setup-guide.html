  • →

    Your name + business email Use the email you check daily. Meta will send important notifications here.

  • →

  • →

  • →

Meta Setup — Step 03

Create Ad Account + Add Payment

This is where your campaigns live and your daily spend gets tracked.

  • →
    Left Menu → Accounts → Ad Accounts → Add → Create a New Ad Account Name it: "Vaquero X Landscape - Ads"
  • →
    Currency: USD / Time Zone: Mountain Time (Arizona) This cannot be changed later. Get it right now.
  • →
    Click "Create Ad Account" Ad account is now live inside your Business Manager.
  • →
    Add Payment Method Ad Account Settings → Payment Settings → Add debit or credit card.
⚠️ Your card is NOT charged until ads go live. Starting budget: $10–$15/day. You can pause or stop anytime from your phone.
Meta Setup — Step 04 (Most Important)

Give CesarScales Full Access

This is how we manage everything on your behalf. You stay in control — you can remove access at any time.

  • →
    Partners → Add → "Give a partner access to your assets" Do NOT click "Ask a partner to share their assets" — wrong direction.
  • →
    Enter CesarScales Business Manager ID We'll show you the exact number during setup.
  • →
    Assign: Facebook Page + Ad Account → Set role: Admin We need Admin on both to build and manage campaigns.
  • →
    Click "Send Request" — we accept immediately Done. We're connected.

Meta Setup — Step 05

Install Meta Pixel on Your Website

Tracks every visitor. Tells your ads who's interested, who filled out a form, and who became a lead — so we optimize for real results.

  • →
    Left Menu → Events Manager Inside your Business Manager.
  • →
    Connect Data Sources → Web → Meta Pixel Name it: "Vaquero X Pixel"
  • →
    Enter your website URL and select install method WordPress = use Meta Pixel plugin + paste Pixel ID. Custom site = paste code before </head>.
  • →
    We handle the install — you just need to give us site access WordPress login or access to your site manager. Takes under 10 minutes.
  • →
    Verify Pixel is live — green dot in Events Manager Confirm within 10 minutes of install. Green = tracking. You're live.
