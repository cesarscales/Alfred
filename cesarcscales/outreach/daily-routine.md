# CesarScales — Daily Outreach Routine
Target: Landscaping business owners across the US
Goal: Book 2–3 calls/week → 1 close/month

---

## Daily Numbers
| Method | Goal |
|---|---|
| Cold DMs (new accounts) | 20–30 |
| Comments on posts | 15–20 |
| Follow + story view (warm up) | 20–30 |
| Cold calls | 10–20 |

---

## Morning Block (30 min) — Before Jobs
**Do this while coffee is brewing or driving to first job.**

1. **Follow 10 new landscaping accounts** — search #landscapingbusiness #lawncare #landscaper
2. **Comment on 10 posts** (use comment scripts)
3. **Check DM replies** — respond to anyone who replied overnight
4. **Send 10 cold DMs** to accounts you followed yesterday

---

## Midday Block (15 min) — Lunch or break
1. **Send 10–15 more cold DMs**
2. **Follow 10 more accounts**
3. **Reply to any new DM responses**

---

## Evening Block (20 min) — After jobs/bartending
1. **Comment on 10 more posts**
2. **Send final 5–10 DMs**
3. **Log results** — who replied, who's interested, who to follow up

---

## How to Find Targets on Instagram
- Search: `#landscapingcompany` `#lawncarebusiness` `#landscaper` `#lawnmaintenance`
- Look for accounts with: 200–5,000 followers, real job photos, no ads running
- Check their bio — if they have a website but no "ads by" mention, they're a target
- Avoid: accounts already running ads, franchises, massive companies

---

## Weekly Goal
- Mon–Fri: Full routine
- Sat: DMs + comments only (lighter day)
- Sun: Off or review only

| Week Target | Number |
|---|---|
| Total DMs sent | 150–200 |
| Replies received | 10–20 |
| Calls booked | 2–3 |
| Closes | 1 every 2–4 weeks to start |
