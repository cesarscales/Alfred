# CesarScales — DM Scripts
Target: Landscaping business owners
Platform: Instagram

---

## RULE: Never pitch in the first DM. Start a conversation. Pitch on reply.

---

## Cold DM — Script 1 (Compliment + Question)
```
Hey [Name] — just came across your page. Your work looks clean, 
especially [mention specific job/photo]. 

Quick question — are you guys running any ads right now to get 
more clients or mostly word of mouth?
```

---

## Cold DM — Script 2 (Direct + Curious)
```
Hey [Name] — I help landscaping companies get consistent leads 
through Facebook and Instagram ads.

Saw your page and honestly think there's a real opportunity for 
you in your area. You currently running any paid ads?
```

---

## Cold DM — Script 3 (Short + Punchy)
```
Hey [Name] — do you guys use Facebook or Instagram ads to get 
new lawn care clients?
```
*Use this when you want volume. Simple, gets a yes/no fast.*

---

## Follow-Up DM (No Reply After 3–5 Days)
```
Hey [Name] — just wanted to bump this up. I work with landscaping 
companies to bring in consistent leads through Meta ads. 

If it's not the right time, no worries — just wanted to make sure 
this didn't get buried.
```

---

## Reply Scripts — When They Say "No we don't run ads"

**Option A — Soft pitch:**
```
Got it — a lot of landscaping companies I work with were in the 
same spot. Most were getting clients through referrals but wanted 
something more consistent.

I run Facebook/Instagram ads specifically for landscaping businesses. 
We target homeowners in your service area and generate inbound leads.

Would it be worth a quick 15-min call to see if it makes sense for you?
```

**Option B — Results first:**
```
Makes sense. Most of my clients didn't run ads before either.

We just started working with a landscaping company in [state] — 
first 30 days, 14 leads, booked 4 recurring clients.

Happy to show you exactly what we did if you're open to a quick call.
```

---

## Reply Scripts — When They Say "Yes we run ads"

```
Nice — are you managing them yourself or working with someone?

I ask because a lot of landscaping companies I talk to are running 
ads but not getting the cost per lead they want. Sometimes a few 
tweaks make a big difference.

What's your current cost per lead looking like?
```

---

## Booking the Call
```
Awesome — I've got time this week. What works better for you, 
[Day] or [Day]? Call takes about 15 minutes, I'll show you 
exactly what we'd run for your area.
```

---

## When They Ghost After Showing Interest
```
Hey [Name] — didn't want to lose touch. Still interested in 
talking ads? No pressure either way — just want to make sure 
I follow up with the right people.
```
